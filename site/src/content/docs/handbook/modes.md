Switch in Settings (Ctrl+, outside the terminal).

## Classic

Classic hides the plan until there is one.

## Guided

Guided keeps the plan column open. The column also opens when Ask is not ready, in either mode. An empty plan says "No plan yet."

## What stays the same

The mode does not change how a command runs, how risk works, what History records, or the shortcuts.
